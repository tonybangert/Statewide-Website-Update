Platform

Everything you need to publish legal notices

Our secure online platform handles the entire legal notice lifecycle.
From submission to verified publication to court-ready affidavits.

Online Submission

Submit notices through our secure portal. Upload, format, and track notices in real-time with automated pricing based on existing rates.

Verified Publication

Every run of every notice is verified for on-time publication. Only placed in newspapers that comply with state statutes.

Affidavit Delivery

Court-ready Affidavits of Publication are automatically emailed after the last run is verified. Original copies delivered when required.

Search & Storage

Search by case number, county, plaintiff, defendant, notice type, or sale date. All details stored online including invoices and affidavits.

System Integration

Integrated with a360inc's CaseAware, proprietary law firm case management systems, and other nationwide CMS platforms to deliver seamless workflow automation, improved efficiency, and maximum cost savings.

Compliance Guaranteed

About Us

Over a decade of legal publishing innovation

Founded in 2014, Statewide Publishing set out to modernize the legal notice industry. We distinguished ourselves from legacy agencies by focusing on superior, error-free execution and placing notices in newspapers of general circulation that are easily accessible to the public.

Today, powered by our strategic partnership with Legal Notice Service (LNS), we operate the first fully connected, compliance-driven legal notice platform in the United States — serving law firms, courts, local governments, and more than 300 Illinois newspaper partners.
